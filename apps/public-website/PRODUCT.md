# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Young professionals (25-35) in Agra — urban, fitness-conscious, social-media-savvy, willing to pay premium for curated experiences. They're looking for a destination that combines sport (pickleball, badminton) with social dining (Cafe Brio), not just a court rental. They browse on mobile first, discover via Instagram/word-of-mouth, and book on impulse when plans align. Corporate event planners (HR, team leads) are a secondary audience for team-building packages.

## Product Purpose

Position The Paddle Club as Agra's premier social-sports destination — the place where competitive play meets artisan dining. The website should make visitors feel the energy and premium quality of the experience before they arrive. Primary conversion: court bookings and cafe reservations. Secondary: corporate event inquiries.

## Brand Personality

Playful, energetic, elite. The brand should feel like walking into a members' club that happens to have world-class courts — confident without being stiff, fun without being juvenile. Voice is direct and punchy, not corporate or overly formal. Visual energy comes from dynamic photography, bold typography, and purposeful motion — not from loud gradients or decorative gimmicks.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (3-column feature cards, cream backgrounds, purple gradients)
- Overly corporate/boring sports facility websites (stock photos, dense text, no personality)
- Cluttered booking sites with overwhelming option grids
- Sites that hide the premium quality behind walls of text
- AI-slop: emoji icons, gradient text, glassmorphism on everything, Inter as default font

## Design Principles

1. **Show the energy, don't describe it** — Real photography and motion convey the vibe; text supports, not leads
2. **Book in 3 taps or less** — Every page funnels toward a booking action; reduce friction relentlessly
3. **Premium is in the details** — Consistent spacing, refined typography, purposeful animations; no cheap shortcuts
4. **One brand, three moods** — Courts (lime/energetic), Cafe (amber/warm), Corporate (blue/professional) each have their own accent but share the same DNA
5. **Dark mode is not optional** — The dark olive-tinted aesthetic IS the brand; light mode would dilute it

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA compliance. Focus on readable contrast ratios (achieved via dark theme), keyboard-navigable booking forms, and reduced-motion alternatives for animations. No specific disability accommodations beyond baseline — the venue itself is not disability-focused.
